Organisations increasingly rely on data (Davenport and Harris 2017, 23). As Porter (1985) argued, competitive advantage
stems from value chains. Teece, Pisano, and Shuen (1997) introduced dynamic capabilities, and later work built on this
(Teece 2007; Eisenhardt and Martin 2000).
