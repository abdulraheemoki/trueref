Leadership and Employee Engagement

Transformational leadership has been linked to higher engagement (Bass and Riggio, 2006). According to Saks (2006), engagement is distinct from commitment. Kahn (1990) first described personal engagement at work. Later research found that "engaged employees are more productive and less likely to leave" (Bakker et al., 2008). However, Schaufeli & Bakker (2004) argue that burnout and engagement are opposites.

The Chartered Institute of Personnel and Development (2023) reports that hybrid work affects engagement. Other studies support this (Smith, 2019; Jones and Brown, 2020, p. 45). Macey and Schneider (2008) proposed a framework, which Rich et al. (2010) tested empirically. Mintzberg (2009, p. 12) states that "management is a practice, not a science or a profession". Finally, Robertson (2017) gives practical advice (CIPD, 2023).
