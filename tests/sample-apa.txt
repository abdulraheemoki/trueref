Digital marketing has changed how firms reach customers (Kotler & Keller, 2016). Chaffey and Ellis-Chadwick (2019) describe the main channels, while Kannan and Li (2017) offer a research framework. Social media engagement drives loyalty (Lemon & Verhoef, 2016; Hollebeek et al., 2014). According to the Office for National Statistics (2022), online sales grew. Some authors (Smith and Lee, 2018) disagree. Ryan states that "digital is no longer a channel but the environment" (Ryan, 2020). Recent work extends this (Kumar, 2021a, 2021b). Ofcom (n.d.) offers data. Dwivedi et al. (2021) review the field.
