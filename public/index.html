      How TrueRef keeps your work private

      Everything happens inside your browser. Your essay and files are never uploaded, saved or tracked. There is no account, no analytics and no database.

      The only time anything goes online is when you choose a DOI lookup or switch on "Look up details online". Then only the reference details go to Crossref, a free public database of published works.

      Want proof? Turn off your Wi-Fi. Checking, fixing and creating references all still work.

      Use TrueRef as an app, even offline

      After your first visit, TrueRef keeps a copy of itself on your device, so it opens even with no internet. Only DOI and Crossref lookups need a connection.

      iPhone or iPad: open TrueRef in Safari, tap the Share button, then "Add to Home Screen".

      Android: open TrueRef in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu, then "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".

      Laptop: in Chrome or Edge, click "Install app" at the top of this page, or the install icon in the address bar.

      TrueRef never rewrites your essay, so it cannot make your writing look AI-generated. It only points out things for you to fix in your own words.

      TrueRef is a helper, not a marker. Always follow your own course or module handbook, as universities adapt referencing styles.
